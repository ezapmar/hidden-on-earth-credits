# Hidden on Earth — Art credits & image sources

Attribution and image provenance for **Hidden on Earth — Seek & Find**, a hidden-object game for kids played on famous paintings.

**Website:** https://ezapmar.github.io/hidden-on-earth-credits/

- [Art credits & sources](https://ezapmar.github.io/hidden-on-earth-credits/) — every painting is a public-domain artwork (CC0 museum open-access or Wikimedia Commons).
- [Privacy Policy](https://ezapmar.github.io/hidden-on-earth-credits/privacy.html) — the app collects no data: no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking, or network connections.

The three pictures in *Your collection* are original works by Dünya Üçer, used with the family's permission.
